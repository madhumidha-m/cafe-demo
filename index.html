Our Story

Coffee is more than
a drink — it's a craft

Roast & Ritual began with a single obsession: what does a truly perfect cup of coffee taste like? We travelled across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Coorg to find out. What we brought back was not just beans — it was a philosophy.

Every batch we roast, every pour we make, carries that original question. Our baristas are trained not just in technique, but in patience — because the best coffee cannot be rushed.
